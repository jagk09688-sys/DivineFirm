Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a housekeeping appointment?

Simply call us on +61 410 093 694 or email admin@divinefirm.online with your home size and preferred schedule. We'll create a tailored plan and confirm your first visit within 1-2 business days.

What areas do you service?

We provide housekeeping services throughout Clyde North and nearby suburbs including Dandenong, Fountain Gate, Cranbourne, and surrounding areas. Contact us to confirm your specific location.

Are your cleaners background checked?

Yes, absolutely. All our housekeeping staff are fully background checked, insured, and vetted. Your home's security and safety is our top priority.

What products do you use?

We use eco-friendly, non-toxic cleaning products that are safe for your family and pets. If you have specific product preferences or allergies, let us know and we'll accommodate them.

Can I change my housekeeping schedule?

Absolutely! You can adjust your schedule anytime—weekly, fortnightly, or monthly visits. Just let us know and we'll adapt your plan to fit your lifestyle.

What's your cancellation policy?

We ask for 48 hours' notice for cancellations. Emergency cancellations can be arranged—just call us directly. Rescheduling is always free of charge.
